About Our Project

We travel to the parts of a place that still surprise its own residents — the night market that only opens on Thursdays, the temple fair that moves with the harvest.

Our Mission

To record living local culture before it changes out from under us — and to do it in a way that respects the people who hold it. Every piece we publish comes from time spent on the ground, with consent and fair payment.

Our Vision

A travel library where a traveller can find not just what to see, but how to behave when they get there. We want the local guide at the corner shop to be as important to the story of a place as the famous monument two hundred kilometres away.

Core Values

  • Consent first: Nothing about a person, ceremony or home is published without an explicit yes.
  • Fair pay: Guides, cooks, drivers and makers are paid directly, at a rate we set in advance.
  • Credit always: Every contributor is named. No anonymous quotes taken from a gate.

What We Offer

Field notes, festival calendars, market guides, artisan interviews and slow-travel routes — plus a trip log where you can keep your own journeys and compare them with other readers.

Behind the Project

Run by writers, photographers and local coordinators who have all lived in the towns they cover. Many of our contributors grew up in the places they now write about, which is the whole point.

Looking Ahead

Next up: a full festival calendar for the monsoon and harvest seasons, audio walk-throughs of four old town quarters, and a directory of family-run guesthouses priced for local budgets. We are also building an archive of oral histories in the original language, with translations made by the speaker rather than by a machine.
