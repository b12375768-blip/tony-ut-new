Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-22

1. Introduction

At Local Culture & Travel (“we”, “us”, or “our”), your privacy matters to us. Our goal is a safe, informative space for travellers who want to understand local culture responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information while you browse, read, or plan a trip across the site.

2. Information We Collect

  • Browsing data: We do not collect personal identifiers. Basic non-personal information (such as browser type or screen size) may be used to keep pages fast and readable.
  • Local preferences: Your journey scores and trip log entries are stored in your browser’s localStorage. That data stays on your device and is never uploaded to us.
  • Contact form: If you send us a planning request, we receive the details you provide (name, email, message) only so we can reply.

3. How We Use Information

  • To keep navigation smooth and our guides easy to read.
  • To improve accessibility and page performance for all readers.
  • To reply to trip requests and reader questions.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Local Culture & Travel does not use tracking cookies or analytics by default. If we introduce analytics or sponsorship measurement in the future, we will update this policy first and keep those tools limited to aggregated page views.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our site suits general audiences, but we recommend that children under 13 browse with a parent or guardian. Travel planning often involves sharing personal details, so parental supervision is the safest approach.

6. Data Retention

Local data (journey scores, trip log entries) stays in your browser until you clear it yourself. Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to reply and maintain a brief planning record.

7. Community Guidelines for Content

We never sell contributor information, and we do not publish details about the people we interview without their explicit, informed consent. If a contributor asks us to remove their material, we remove it.

8. Your Choices

  • Clear or delete saved journey data using your browser’s site-data settings.
  • Browse in private or incognito mode for sessions that do not save local data.
  • Ask us to delete a message you previously sent from our records.

9. Third-Party Links

We may reference official tourism offices, cultural institutions or media archives. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices once you leave our pages.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep browsing safe. No online platform, however, can promise complete security across the internet.

11. International Visitors

Our site is read worldwide. By visiting, you consent to information being processed in the country where our servers operate, under this Privacy Policy.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as features change or requirements shift. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the most recent revision.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or data use? Reach us through the contact section of the site and we will respond promptly.
