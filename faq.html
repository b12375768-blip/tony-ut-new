FAQ

Everything you need to know before, during and after a trip with us.

What does Local Culture & Travel actually cover?

We focus on living culture rather than monuments — food, festivals, craft, music and the everyday routines that make a town itself. We currently document more than 240 small towns and market districts across the regions on our routes.

Do you run packaged tours or book travel for me?

No. We are a publication and a planning resource. We do not sell tickets or package trips. What we do is tell you who to contact locally, what a fair price looks like, and how to arrange things directly with residents and family-run operators.

How do you choose the people you interview?

Through local coordinators, residents and repeat visitors. Most of our contributors grew up in the places they write about. Anyone can be suggested through the contact form, and we verify with a second local source before publishing.

Do contributors get paid?

Yes. Writers, photographers, translators and cooks we commission are paid a fixed rate agreed in advance, and contributors whose material we reuse receive a continuing credit with a reuse fee where one is due.

What is your rule on photography?

Always ask, always wait for a clear answer, and always accept a no without argument. We do not publish photographs of ceremonies, faces or private interiors without an explicit yes from the people in them.

How do I know a festival date is correct?

Many local festivals follow crop cycles and lunar calendars, so printed dates are often out of date. Check our field notes first, then confirm with the local temple committee, panchayat office or tourism desk two to three weeks before you travel.

Is the site free to use?

Yes. All guides, field notes and the trip log are free. We may carry clearly marked sponsorship in the future, and it will never influence what we recommend — a paid placement does not buy a listing.

What is the Trip Log on this site?

It is a place where readers record journeys they have taken using our guides. Entries are stored in your own browser, so nothing is uploaded and nothing is shared with us unless you choose to send it.

Can I submit a place, dish or festival?

Please do. Use the contact form on the home page and include the town and, if possible, someone local we can verify the detail with. We read every submission.

Do you publish itineraries with fixed days?

We publish routes with a suggested order and generous time in each place, not hour-by-hour schedules. Fixed timetables fight the pace of a real town, and they are the reason people end up rushing the best part.

What languages are your guides available in?

Each field note lists the languages available for a walk or interview, plus whether a translator is needed. For less widely spoken languages we work with local translators rather than machine translation.

Is it safe to travel to the places you cover?

We publish current practical notes on transport, access and local conditions in every guide, and we update them regularly. We do not give legal or medical advice — your government’s travel advisory is the right source for that.
