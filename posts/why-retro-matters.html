Slow Routes: Linking Four Towns in a Week

Fewer stops, more time, better stories.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Fewer stops, more time, better stories.

The eleven-stop version

Our original southern route covered eleven towns in seven days. It produced a lot of photographs and almost no conversation, because every stop lasted a morning.

The four-stop version

Cut to four towns with two full days each. Same transport corridor, roughly the same cost, and three conversations we had previously rushed past entirely.

Fewer bookings, less admin

Four guesthouses is manageable. Eleven is a spreadsheet. The route got cheaper and calmer the moment we cut it.

How to cut your own route

Rank every stop by how much you want to be there, not by how many photos you expect. Keep four. Give the rest to the next trip.
