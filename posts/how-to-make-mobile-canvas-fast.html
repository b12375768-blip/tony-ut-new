Planning Around the Rainy Months

What to do when half the town shuts for an afternoon.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

What to do when half the town shuts for an afternoon.

Rain changes the schedule

Outdoor festivals, boat trips and hill walks are seasonal. Build at least two spare days into a route that runs in the monsoon so a washout does not cost you the trip.

Rainy-day economies

In wet seasons, towns shift indoors: covered bazaars, long eating houses, workshops where the work never stopped. These are the months locals recommend for craft visits.

Clothing and shoes

One waterproof layer, one pair of shoes that tolerate standing water, and a dry bag for phones and documents. This is the whole kit.

Ask what everyone else does

In wet-season towns, the same two indoor activities fill every weekend. Locals will tell you within one message, and it will save you a wasted day.
