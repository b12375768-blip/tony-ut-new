Before Sunrise at the Covered Market

A 5am walk through a market that only wakes up once a week.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

A 5am walk through a market that only wakes up once a week.

Why the early hour matters

The market has been trading for two hours before the sun clears the roofline. Stallholders know who buys the best produce and at what hour, and if you arrive at nine you are already late for the interesting part.

Bring nothing, ask for a hand

Do not carry a basket unless offered. The most useful thing a visitor can do is pick up a crate of chillies or hold a folding table while someone ties the shade cloth. Ten minutes of that buys you an hour of conversation.

Eat standing at the counter

Breakfast is not a restaurant decision, it is a thirty-minute window. Order whatever the person in front of you ordered, add a second thing you do not recognise, and eat it standing up like everyone else.

Pay the posted price

If you are unsure, ask what it costs before you order. Prices are almost always posted and almost always honest. Getting this wrong is the fastest way to make a vendor stop talking to you.
