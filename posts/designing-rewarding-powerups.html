Reading a Festival Programme Like a Local

Order of events, unwritten rules and where to stand.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Order of events, unwritten rules and where to stand.

The programme is not the schedule

Printed programmes list events, not order. The procession goes first because it always goes first, whatever the leaflet says.

Stand at the edge, early

The best viewing is not the front. It is two rows back on a corner where the procession turns and you can see faces as well as the float.

Know the photo blackout

Some parts of a ceremony are explicitly not photographed, and locals watch. Our default is no camera inside a ritual, ever.

Stay for the clean-up

The twenty minutes after the drums stop, when people carry the chairs away, is where you actually understand the size of what just happened.
