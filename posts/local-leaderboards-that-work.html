Ranking the Ten Best Meals in a Town

Our method, spelled out, so you can repeat it yourself.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Our method, spelled out, so you can repeat it yourself.

Three visits across two weeks

We do not rank a stall on one lunch. Two weeks also lets a seasonal dish return, which is often the reason people come.

Three scores, equally weighted

How good the food is, how fairly it is priced, and how the vendor treats a first-time customer. A brilliant dish served rudely ranks lower, and should.

Weight local frequency last and highest

How often a stall has a queue of people who work nearby tells you more than any review. We use it as the deciding tie-breaker.

Publish the method

A ranking without a method is an opinion. The method is the part you can check, and the part you can repeat in a town we have not covered.
