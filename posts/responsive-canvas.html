Old Towns Without the Souvenir Shops

Where the shops still sell what the street actually needs.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Where the shops still sell what the street actually needs.

Look for repair shops

A town with a working cobbler, tailor and hardware store is a town that still functions for its residents. That is the neighbourhood worth walking, not the one with the postcard racks.

The pharmacy is the landmark

One busy pharmacy, one large temple and one primary school usually mark the centre of real daily life. Orient yourself there and walk outward.

Empty upper floors are normal

In many old towns the ground floor is commerce and the upper floors are homes. Do not read an empty doorway as a dead street; ask before assuming you cannot enter.

Ask before photographing shopfronts

Owners working in their doorway are working. A quick gesture with the camera held low, then a step back if they shake their head, resolves it without awkwardness.
