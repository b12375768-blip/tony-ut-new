The Ten Stalls Worth Queueing For

A ranking built on repeat visits, not one good lunch.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

A ranking built on repeat visits, not one good lunch.

Three visits minimum

A single good meal proves nothing. Weather, season and who cooked that day all matter. Our rule is three visits across at least two weeks before a stall makes this list.

Queue length is a signal, not a warning

A stall with six people waiting is usually better than an empty one next door. Locals are a more reliable filter than any printed recommendation.

Eat what is in season

Ask what came in from where this morning. The best thing on the menu is usually whatever arrived first and will not last.

How to be a good customer

Order one thing, not four. Finish it. Do not ask for changes. Come back the same week and you will be treated differently than a tourist who tried everything once.
