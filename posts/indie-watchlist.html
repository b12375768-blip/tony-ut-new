What actually earns its place in your bag.

Our list halved in a year

Twelve items became six. Everything removed was either duplicated by something local or unused on four separate trips.

What we regret leaving

A second pair of shoes you can walk ten kilometres in, and a small roll of tape that solves luggage, sandals, gift-wrapping and broken straps.

Buy heavy things where they are made

A ceramic bowl or a scarf is lighter in your luggage than the object itself bought at home, and it comes with a story you can tell.

Pack for the laundry line

Most towns have laundry service for less than a coffee. Two base layers and a bar of soap removes a third of your bag.
