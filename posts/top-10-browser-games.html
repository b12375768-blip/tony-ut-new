Ten Towns Where the Festival Is the Reason to Go

Timing, cost and what to book before you travel.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Timing, cost and what to book before you travel.

Book transport first

Festival transport is the first thing to sell out. Sort the journey before you sort the stay, and book the festival date only after the route works.

Stay near but not in it

Rooms within the festival ground are noisy and expensive. Ten minutes away on foot is almost always better and always cheaper.

Check what is cancelled

Weather cancellations are common for outdoor events. Keep the second and third days of your trip unplanned so a cancellation does not ruin the visit.

Come for the day after

The morning after a festival is the quietest, friendliest time to visit a town. Most people do not, which is exactly why it is good.
