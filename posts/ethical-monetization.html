Meeting the Makers

Pottery, boat building and the economics behind both.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Pottery, boat building and the economics behind both.

Three workshops, three prices

We visited a potter selling at a city market, a boat builder working to order, and a cooper who supplies local taverns. Only two of them earn what their time costs.

The middleman problem

Tours often buy cheap and resell high while presenting the craft as authentic. Buying at the workshop is usually cheaper for you and better for the maker.

What a fair rate looks like

Ask for the price for a small piece you can carry. If the maker seems embarrassed, that is your cue to offer more, not less.

Why slow crafts are disappearing

Not lack of interest, but lack of margin. When a day of weaving is worth less than a day of retail, the tradition does not survive on goodwill alone.
