What to check before booking, and what to ask when you arrive.

Surface and steps

Old towns are cobbled and stepped in ways no listing mentions. Ask the guesthouse for the nearest vehicle-accessible street rather than the nearest landmark.

Toilets and water

Availability changes seasonally. Confirm in writing before you travel, then confirm again on arrival. Bring a small backup for long village routes.

Transport that works

Not every line reaches every town. We publish the bus times that locals use, including the ones a visitor would never find on a map.

Ask the guesthouse first

Family-run stays usually know what a traveller with limited mobility needs and can solve it on the day. That conversation is free and takes two minutes.
