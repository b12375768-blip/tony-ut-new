Learning Ten Words That Change Everything

The small vocabulary that makes a trip feel different.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

The small vocabulary that makes a trip feel different.

Ten is genuinely enough

Greetings, please, thank you, excuse me, water, food, how much, where, toilet and goodbye. That set covers most of a first week and buys real goodwill everywhere.

Learn the price numerals

Numbers are usually the words people use most, and hearing them in the local script makes shopping dramatically less painful.

Pronunciation is politeness

Attempting a word badly is read as effort, which is appreciated. Never attempting it is read as indifference. There is no penalty for trying.

Ask a child, thank an elder

If you want a word explained, children will teach you and elders will appreciate being asked. Both conversations cost nothing and both are remembered.
