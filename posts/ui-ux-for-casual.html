Photography with Consent

The only etiquette rule that is not negotiable.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

The only etiquette rule that is not negotiable.

Ask a specific person, not the crowd

Shouting "can I take a photo?" at a group produces no answer. Ask one person standing nearest, quietly, so they can answer without an audience.

Wording that works

"Is it alright if I take a photo of you?" with the camera held low, not raised. Low means you have not committed yet, and the person can refuse without you having already framed the shot.

What to do after a no

Say thank you, put the camera away, and carry on normally. No apology speech. Most people who decline are relieved it was simple.

Where consent is implied and where it is not

A market stall selling openly is usually fine at a distance. A ceremony, a religious interior or a private home is not, no matter how photogenic the light is.
