How to Behave at a Temple Fair

Etiquette, timing and the one rule everyone gets wrong.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

Etiquette, timing and the one rule everyone gets wrong.

Remove your shoes, properly

Not just at the entrance, but again before the inner shrine, even if nobody is watching. People notice, and it is the fastest signal that you came from somewhere else.

The direction of the flow

Processions move in a set direction. Walking against it, or standing in the middle of the route to take a photograph, will block the whole lane. Move to the edge, then take the shot.

Ask before you photograph a ceremony

Not once, but every time. Ask a specific person standing beside you, not the whole crowd. If the answer is uncomfortable or unclear, treat it as no.

What to bring, what to leave

Water, a cloth, small notes and nothing else. Skip the backpack if you can. Temples are crowded, and a bag you have to guard is a bag that stops you from paying attention.
