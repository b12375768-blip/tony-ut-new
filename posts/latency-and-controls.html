Slow Travel on a Budget Without Missing Anything

The trade-offs we actually made, and what they cost.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

The trade-offs we actually made, and what they cost.

Stay three nights, not one

One night means paying full price to do everything. Three nights in one town is where the second and third days happen, and those are the good ones.

Overnight trains beat day trains

Sleeping on the journey replaces a hotel night and covers ground while you rest. It is the single most effective budget move on our routes.

Eat where the workers eat

The lunch counter opposite a construction site or factory gate beats the hotel restaurant at a third of the cost and twice the interest.

Buy the museum pass last

It only pays off if you visit four or more sites. Three sites and a good market afternoon beats a rushed circuit.
