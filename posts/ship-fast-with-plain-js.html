A Two-Week Language Plan That Works

How much you can realistically pick up, and when.

Field Note — Published 2025-08-22

How much you can realistically pick up, and when.

Week one is listening only

Do not open a grammar book. Record ten minutes of ordinary speech a day and replay it before bed. Recognition arrives before production, always.

Week two is output

Ten phrases, spoken badly and often. Order food, ask for directions, ask a question, thank someone. Repetition beats accuracy at this stage.

Use it or lose it

A traveller who speaks on day one and waits until day six gets two days of value. Speak from the first morning, even with gestures.

Find one conversation

One regular conversation a week is worth more than a phrasebook. Local language hours exist for exactly this, and they are free.
