Plan a Trip

Everything we offer is free. What we do is point you to the right local people and tell you what a fair arrangement looks like.

Route Planning

Tell us your dates, your pace and what you actually enjoy. We reply with a route that leaves room to wander, not a packed schedule.

Festival Calendar

Seasonal dates, harvest timings and the local contacts who can confirm what is happening this month. We update it as dates shift.

Local Guide Directory

Independent guides, cooks and drivers, listed with the rates we agreed and the languages they speak. Book them directly.

Family-Run Guesthouses

Small stays priced for local budgets, listed with the owner, not a booking platform. Direct contact only.

Artisan & Workshop Bookings

Arrange a visit to a weaving, pottery or boat-building workshop. Makers set their own terms and are paid in full.

Photography & Consent Notes

Per-route guidance on what can be photographed, and the exact wording we use to ask. Print it out before you leave.

How a Planning Request Works

  • Step 1: Send your dates, budget and interests through the contact form.
  • Step 2: A coordinator replies with a suggested route and local contacts.
  • Step 3: You book and pay local hosts directly. We take no commission.
  • Step 4: Tell us what we got wrong so the next traveller benefits.

Ready when you are

Send us your dates and we will suggest a first route within a few days.
